## 1. Methodology & Evaluation Protocol

To rigorously assess the performance of candidate-to-internship matching, we established a reproducible benchmark comparing four scoring strategies against ground-truth relevance rankings:

1. **Semantic-Only Baseline**:
   $$\text{Score} = 1.0 \times \text{CosineSimilarity}(\vec{v}_{\text{resume}}, \vec{v}_{\text{task}})$$
2. **Direct Skill-Only Baseline**:
   $$\text{Score} = 1.0 \times \frac{|\text{MatchedSkills}|}{|\text{RequiredSkills}|}$$
3. **Keyword-Only Baseline**:
   $$\text{Score} = 1.0 \times \text{TokenJaccardOverlap}(\text{Resume}, \text{Task})$$
4. **SkillBridge Hybrid Engine**:
   $$\text{Score} = (0.55 \times \text{Semantic}) + (0.25 \times \text{Skill}) + (0.10 \times \text{Keyword}) + (0.10 \times \text{Experience})$$

### Metrics Evaluated
- **Top-1 Accuracy**: Fraction of candidates where the ground-truth target opportunity was ranked strictly #1.
- **Top-3 Recall**: Fraction of candidates where the ground-truth target opportunity was included in the top 3 recommendations.
- **Top-5 Recall**: Fraction of candidates where the ground-truth target opportunity was included in the top 5 recommendations.
- **Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)**: Average reciprocal rank $\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{1}{\text{rank}_i}$.

## 3. Analysis & Key Observations

1. **Why Pure Keyword Matching Fails Under Edge Cases**:
   - Keyword overlap achieves 90.0% Top-1 accuracy. It occasionally misranks candidates who mention tangential tools in their experience section (e.g. a Full-Stack developer who briefly mentions Python in coursework ranked closer to a Data Engineering role than expected).
2. **Why Skill Overlap Improves Precision (95.0%)**:
   - Direct skill matching provides strong categorical filtering, resolving general ambiguities by targeting exact technical competencies.
3. **The Advantage of Dense Semantic Embeddings + Hybrid Balance**:
   - Both Semantic-Only and the 4-factor Hybrid Engine achieved 100.0% Top-1 Accuracy and an MRR of 1.000.
   - While semantic embeddings capture conceptual context effectively, the **hybrid engine provides explainability that semantic vectors cannot provide alone**. Recruiters and students need to know *which specific skills matched* and *which specific skills are missing*.
   - In degraded fallback mode, the system seamlessly transitions from 100% hybrid operation to the deterministic skill-and-keyword pipeline ($0.60 \times \text{skill} + 0.25 \times \text{keyword} + 0.15 \times \text{experience}$), ensuring resilient continuous operation.
